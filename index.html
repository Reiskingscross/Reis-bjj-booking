Data Protection & GDPR

Why we collect your data: To process your booking request, ensure participant safety, and meet our legal obligations as a sports club.

What we collect: Name, date of birth, contact details, health information, and emergency contact details. Health data is special category data under UK GDPR, processed only with your explicit consent.

Third parties: Your data is processed by Formspree (email delivery). Formspree is a GDPR-compliant processor.

Retention: Data is kept for up to 3 years, or 7 years for waiver records. Contact info@reisbjj.co.uk to request deletion at any time.

I give explicit consent for Reis BJJ Kings Cross to collect and process my personal and health data as described above, in accordance with UK GDPR.
